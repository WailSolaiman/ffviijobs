# Final Fantasy VII (1997) — Job System & Character Classes

Custom rules for the original international game. Equipment lists are permissions, not simultaneous loadouts. All names use English in-game terminology.

## Rules

- Materia are character-exclusive except Restore, Revive, Heal, Full Cure, Barrier, Shield, Time and Enemy Skill, as listed below.
- The healing permissions apply throughout the game, before and after Aerith leaves the party. Both Blue Mages may use all 24 Enemy Skills.
- Master Command (Cloud), Master Magic (Red XIII), and Master Summon (Vincent) are explicit exceptions to ability and summon exclusivity. Other characters cannot equip them.
- Accessories may be shared only among the characters listed. Original accessory allowances are retained, including stat accessories such as Earring, Power Wrist and Talisman; new shared allowances are defensive or time-themed.
- No universal Materia or accessories. Duplicate copies do not change ownership. Normal item use, weapons and armor are unrestricted by this document.
- A dash means no Materia of that category is assigned. These are self-imposed rules; the original game does not enforce jobs.

## Design decisions and gameplay caveats

- Cloud keeps the original elemental sword identity; Fire, Ice and Lightning remain his exclusive base spells. Vincent receives the advanced offensive spells and summon support. Red XIII uses Earth and can eventually access every spell through Master Magic.
- Barret receives Underwater as the tank for Emerald Weapon; Cait Sith receives Chocobo Lure as the Choco/Mog user; Yuffie receives Gil Plus and encounter avoidance as a thief; Tifa receives EXP Plus as a martial training bonus.
- All is assigned to Aerith, as in the original sheet. When she leaves, it is no longer usable under these rules. Red XIII and Cait Sith can still heal/revive individual targets and use group healing via Enemy Skill (e.g. White Wind). No automatic reassignment is assumed.
- Cid has no Enemy Skill under the agreed exclusivity rule; his dragon identity is expressed through the three Bahamut summons, Long Range and MP Absorb (linked to compatible summons). HP Absorb goes to Red XIII for nature-themed sustain, not Cid.
- Barret can link Final Attack to a compatible Materia such as his summon, but cannot use the classic Final Attack + Phoenix combination because Phoenix belongs to Vincent. Final Attack does not itself revive the party.
- Aerith retains Alexander and all her own exclusive permissions until her departure; thereafter those exclusive items cannot be equipped. The healing exceptions remain available to the surviving characters.
- Master Materia may grant abilities allocated elsewhere. This is intentional, including the ability for Vincent to invoke other characters’ summons via Master Summon.
- Cure, Regen, Life and Osmose are not separate equippable Materia. The actual Materia names are used rather than the original sheet’s spell names.
- This distribution is a role-based design, not a tested balance patch. No requirement to obtain every item before a particular story event.
